The 5 survival needs

Food • Water • Air • Protection • Shelter

When a habitat changes, ask: Which of these needs became harder to get?

The test-writer trick

Many answer choices sound possible. Pick the one that is best supported by the habitat clue.

Location + soil/water + plant feature + animal adaptation = strong evidence.

Power sentence

“This organism can thrive because its feature helps it get what it needs in that habitat.”

Use this sentence frame when explaining your reasoning aloud.
